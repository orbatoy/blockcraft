Crafting & Guide

Break a natural block to collect it – it becomes unlimited in your block bar. Everything below must be crafted (and is counted). Coal & iron need a stone pickaxe; gems (deep underground and in cave walls) need an iron pickaxe. Breaking a crafted block gives it back.

* Natural blocks are only needed once collected – they are never used up.

How to Play

Move – drag a finger on the left side. Keyboard: WASD / arrows. Space or ↑ to jump (hold it to swim).

Look – drag on the right side of the screen. Mouse: click, then move.

Break – tap a block, or hold the Break button. Mouse: left click. Poke animals to make them run.

Place – pick a block on the bar, then press Place. Mouse: right click. Keys 1–8.

Saves each minute if something changed. Collected blocks are unlimited; tap Craft for recipes and the guide. Zombies and spiders roam at night! Double-tap Jump to fly. Break animals for meat, press Eat (E). Torch = slot 9. Face a door or chest and press Place to open it; push forward on a ladder to climb.
